VYOMSENTINEL Hero Video
=======================

Place your hero video file here as: hero.mp4

Requirements:
- Format: MP4 (H.264 codec recommended)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 or higher
- Duration: 10-30 seconds (will loop)
- Content: Space, orbital, or satellite footage

If no video is present, the application will generate a procedural
fallback texture with orbital lines and gradient effects.
The scene will never break due to a missing video file.
